Recruiting Scoreboard

Sep to Oct 2026

A personal operating system for a job search, kept current by six scheduled AI agents.

Most job searches fail on follow-through, not on ideas. The board shows one next action, records what I actually did, and resurfaces technical questions I missed after 1, 3 and 7 days. A resume agent tailors my resume to each firm without changing its format.

Holds
Every role, contact, follow-up, interview and firm brief in one web app on a shared database
Daily loop
One task, a 5-minute market brief, graded drills that unlock the next task
Status
